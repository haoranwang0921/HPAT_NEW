## Ready With Limitations

- The paper-facing reasonable-high boundary combines traced Linear/MVM and 1x1-convolution activity with shape-derived attention products; spatial/depthwise convolution remains electronic.
- Amdahl-style end-to-end bounds quantify how the electronic remainder limits whole-model speedup even under optimistic optical speedup assumptions.
- Energy uncertainty sweeps use an unnormalized explicit-unit-cost local model and expose component sensitivity; they are not calibrated silicon energy.
- P0-E3 is a paper-eligible-with-limitations fixed-subset local/modelled diagnostic; it does not establish physical optical-operator or full-ImageNet robustness.
- P0 import/provenance gates are implemented for simulator activity, edge/mobile baseline logs, dataset-coupled non-ideality accuracy, and desktop baseline provenance; blocked rows remain non-claimable.
